A storyteller first — a CEO by calling

Tlove Media Concept was founded on a simple observation: Nigeria's most beautiful moments — weddings, concerts, conferences — deserved media coverage as vibrant as the events themselves.

What began as a passion for cameras and storytelling has grown into a full creative house covering event planning and coverage, graphics design, social media marketing, content writing and production — with ventures in real estate and insurance that bring the same care for presentation and trust.

The CEO leads each production personally, insisting that every frame, flyer and film carries the Tlove signature: planned with precision, delivered with warmth.
